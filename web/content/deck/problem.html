The problem

Three things break when real‑world assets go on‑chain

01

Compliance exposes every holder

Permissioned assets rely on public allowlists. Every KYC’d wallet, and every fund position, is visible to anyone.

02

Equity oracles pause for the weekend

iAsset marks freeze from Friday close to Monday open while trading continues. The Monday gap lands on traders and insurance funds.

03

Agents hold the whole wallet

AI trading agents are handed full signing power, with no on-chain limit on what they can do and no audit trail.
