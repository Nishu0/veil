The solution

One account, three guarantees

Private compliance

A zero-knowledge proof shows KYC passed, accredited, jurisdiction allowed. The chain stores a nullifier and an expiry, never a name.

Permissioned RWA vault

Deposits mint vRWA, backed by tokenized T-bills and an iAsset perp hedge. Transfers to unverified wallets are refused.

Bounded risk agent

It trades only through a spend-capped, expiring mandate, cuts leverage before the weekend freeze, and hashes every decision on-chain.

Why Injective

MultiVM tokens, chain-level permission hooks, an exchange precompile, iAssets and native scoped authorizations, together on one chain.
